FAQ

Before you order

How do I place an order?

Open the piece you like, choose the size and fill in your details. We call you to confirm the price and the delivery date.

What sizes do you have?

Every piece comes in small, medium and large. You will find the sizes on each product's page.

How do I pay?

After we confirm the price with you, you pay with InstaPay to dedro@instapay, or by scanning the code that appears after you send your order.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the piece and the size. We confirm the date with you before work begins.
